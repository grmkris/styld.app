will break. Every problem you find helps, so please report it, however small.

## What to try

The tabs are **Home**, **Ask**, **Wardrobe** and **Boards**; **Account** is under your initials. Try each part on
the phone and on a computer.

**Adding clothes** (the **+** or **Add clothes**)

- **Photos:** choose one or more photos from your phone. Each piece is cut out of its background.
- **Hold pieces up:** prop the phone up and hold one garment after another in front of the camera; it takes the
  picture by itself when you hold still, front first, then the back.
- **Lay pieces out:** lay clothes on the floor or a bed and photograph them together; each piece becomes its own card.
- **A video:** film your clothes slowly from about a metre away, one by one (up to 15 minutes). A piece seen several
  times should appear once.
- **From a shop's page** (Ask › Add from a link): paste a product link for something you own. Some shops refuse; then
  Styld asks for a photo.

**The review tray** (**To review**)

- Every new piece waits here first. Keep it, untick what is not yours, or keep the original photo instead of the
  cut-out. Check that a pair of shoes shows as one piece, and whether "Already in your wardrobe?" is right.

**Your wardrobe** (Wardrobe › Pieces)

- Browse by group (clothing, shoes, accessories) and type, and try the filters (colour, season, occasion, brand).
- Check that your old Styld pieces look right: the right type, colour and picture.

**A piece's page** (tap a piece)

- See the front and add or replace the back. Switch between the clean picture and your photo; try **Redraw** with a
  hint, and **Make it turn** (it takes about two minutes). Edit the name, type and details, or delete it.

**Today** (Home)

- Today's outfit is made from your own pieces. Press **Spin for another outfit** to see others and **Wear this** to
  note that you wore it. Look at **Pick up where you left off**.

**Looks**

- A look is a saved outfit. Make one by saving a board's collage, by asking Styld or your assistant, or with **Wear it
  again** on a photo. Open, share or delete it.

**Boards and collages** (Boards)

- Start a **New board**, pin pieces, looks and pictures, and lay them out on the collage: move, pinch, turn, add text
  and pictures, undo, and **Save as a look**. Use **Save to board** from a piece, look or photo.

**Stickers and mood boards** (on a board: **Make with Styld**)

- **A sticker:** describe a small drawing ("a red umbrella"); it is cut out for the collage and marked as generated.
- **A mood board:** write one sentence about a feeling; Styld picks your own pieces, pins them and lays them out.

**My Gallery** (Wardrobe › Gallery)

- **Add a photo** of an outfit you wore and liked. Styld finds the clothes and asks whether each is one of your
  pieces. Try **Favourite**, **Wear it again**, **Save to board** and **Cut out the outfit**, and offering a missing
  piece to your wardrobe.

**Styld, your stylist** (Ask › Styld, your stylist)

- Ask about your own clothes: "What should I wear today?", "What goes well together?", "Save that as a look". Styld
  shows pieces in its answers. Try it in Slovenian too. Replies can take up to a minute.

**Your public page and What's new**

- In Account › Public profile, choose a username, add a name, city and bio, and **Make it public**. Then make a board
  or a look public with **Share** and open its link signed out (or send it to the other tester). Your wardrobe itself
  stays private.
- On Home, **What's new** shows what people shared: open the other tester's board from it, follow them (their new things
  then come first), and save one of their boards. Report and block exist too; please only try them on each other.
- Tap **What to wear today**: the wheel stops on one of your pieces. Try **Make outfits with it**, **Wear it today**
  and **Build my own**, and spin again a few times.

**ChatGPT and Claude**

- Account › **Use Styld in ChatGPT or Claude** shows the address to paste:
  `https://styldappv2-styldedge-preview-le7xsbk4pgfxkm7o.kristjan-grm11775.workers.dev/mcp`.
- **Claude:** Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, name it Styld, paste the address.
- **ChatGPT:** in settings turn on Developer mode (Apps → Advanced), create an app with OAuth and paste the address.
- Sign in to Styld when asked and approve the connection. Then ask it to find a piece, add a piece from a photo, make
  a look or arrange a board.

## How to report a problem

Send it to Kris (a message is fine). Please include:

1. **Where:** the page, and what you pressed just before.
2. **Device:** phone or computer, and the browser (Safari, Chrome…).
3. **Language:** English or Slovenian.
4. **When:** the date and the time, roughly to the minute (it helps us find it in our logs).
5. **What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.**
6. **A screenshot or a short screen recording**, if you can.

Never send your sign-in code, a password or anything you would not want stored. If a problem is about how something
looks or reads, a screenshot alone is enough.
