# Plan: Eva's Home — What to wear today, What's new, Challenges (soon) — and no Explore page (4 November 2026)

## Context

Eva sketched the signed-in first page on paper and talked it through (video, 4 November 2026; sketch and Slovenian
transcript kept privately in `~/private/styld-research/eva-home-sketch-2026-11-04/`). Top to bottom: "What to wear
today" (a tombola spins over the whole screen and lands on one of your pieces, "today wear this", and from it you
build), "What's new" (new boards, outfits and looks other people made or posted, "like Facebook or Instagram, so you
can scroll"), today's outfit card ("really good, leave it"), a row of challenges ("30 days of red", "Witch October",
"1 item full week"), and Pick up where you left off.

On 2 November she had deferred the tombola and challenges (F-46). The owner now takes the tombola and What's new in,
shows challenges only as "coming soon", and drops the Explore page (Kris, 4 November). v1 had the same wheel (OU-11,
`~/code/stylelab/apps/web/src/lib/item-of-the-day.ts`), parked in `docs/ROADMAP.md`; it comes back here.

## Owner decisions (4 November)

- **What's new**: everyone's public things that passed the check, **people you follow first**; replaces Home's
  "From people you follow".
- **Layout**: the date stays the page title at the top; under it a full-width **"What to wear today"** button, then a
  sideways **strip** of the newest, today's card, Challenges, Pick up, and **"What's new, earlier"** loading more as
  you scroll.
- **Tombola**: a **wheel of your pieces** (~12 cut-outs turn, slow, stop with one at the top, which grows into the
  middle; ≈2.5 s; at once under reduced motion). **Spin again** as often as you like, free.
- After it lands: **Make outfits with it**, **Wear it today**, **Build my own**, **Open the piece**.
- **Challenges**: "coming soon" only. **Explore page goes.** Fix Ask's "Start a board" (found while mapping).

## Assumptions (stated, not asked)

- Challenges: an h2 "Challenges" with a "Coming soon" chip (DESIGN forbids middle-dot heads), Eva's three as quiet
  cards; no links, nothing focusable, nothing stored.
- The button is a secondary pill with a dice icon (Wear this stays the one primary); a skeleton while pieces load;
  shown only with ≥ 2 eligible pieces.
- Eligible: ready, has `images`, suits the day's season (no seasons = all year), `slotOf ≠ set`, `groupOf ≠ sport`,
  not socks; if none suit the season, any eligible. The wheel shows `cdn.piece(…, 'thumb')` (the shown picture).
- Wear it today: today's card re-made around the piece (`anchorPieceId`), "Outfits around {piece}" with "Back to
  today's outfits"; the anchor is never offered for a swap; a new day drops it.
- Build my own: a board "{piece}: my board" with the piece on its table, opened.
- Desktop (≥ 900 px): head and button on one row, the strip full width, the outfit beside Pick up / earlier. Eva's
  sketch is phone-only; she checks it.
- `/explore` lands on Home (address replaced with `/`). Signed out: landing and public pages stay; the top bar's
  Explore link and the landing's "See all" go; a profile's Back goes Home. Ask keeps searching clothes, people,
  boards. Server Explore (`/api/public/explore`, `explore_get`, curation) and board moods stay.
- Pins appear in What's new only when added after the board went public. Re-publishing a board bumps it (known,
  recorded in STATUS).
